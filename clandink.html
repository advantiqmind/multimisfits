Clan Dink Settings

Copy the clan's Dink plugin settings and import them into RuneLite. This keeps everyone's notifications synced to our chest channel.

  1. Click Copy Settings below
  2. Open RuneLite, go to the Dink plugin config
  3. Click the Import button at the bottom
  4. Paste and confirm
