Clan FAQ

How do I join?

Hit the Join button, hop in our Discord, and say hi, and a rank will sort you out. That's the whole process. No applications, no requirements.

Can't see any of the channels?

Make sure you finish the onboarding and check off that you've read the rules in Channels & Rules. Everything will open up after that.

Why haven't I gotten ranked up yet?

Have you changed your Discord nickname to your in-game name? That's the first step.

When you recruit someone, make sure they know we have a Discord. If they mention who recruited them in the recruitment channel, that's the best way for us to keep track. You can even post it yourself.

We do rank-ups once a month and there is limited supply. See the ranking system below for more details.

Do all members use Discord?

No. Not all active members are part of the Discord. This is where we communicate, run events, and manage rankings, but you're not required to use it. However, you will not progress in rank without being in here, so spread the word.

How is my recruiter credited?

Members should post who recruited them in the recruitment channel. The format:

  • Your RSN
  • Who recruited you
  • A screenshot (if possible)

This ensures recruiters receive proper credit and rewards.

How do event donations work?

Event donations are optional. Donations help grow prize pools, but no one is pressured to contribute. Simply put however much you'd like to donate into the clan coffer in the clan hall.
